which are missing — i.e. what **should** be available versus what **is**.

Generated by enumerating the lab drive, not by reading the protocol. ✓ = present,
— = absent. "in dataset" means the session reaches `all_data_05.csv`.

## What the gaps mean

### Study 2 — the three excluded sessions are recoverable

`2425`, `4041` and `6263` have **complete raw data**: two participant videos, two
participant audio tracks, a merged audio track, and both CORAE annotation files.
What they are missing is everything downstream — no WAV conversion, no OpenFace, no
openSMILE, no VAD log, no merged feature table. The pipeline was simply never run on
them.

The published papers attribute the 30 → 27 drop to "hardware inconsistencies" and
"connection problems". The `Observations` column of `ParticipantLog.xlsx` is more
specific, and all three reasons are about **recording quality, not missing files**:

| Session | Logged reason |
|---|---|
| `2425` | a participant video went missing mid-session; the recording is split into `part1` / `part2` |
| `4041` | large glasses occlude much of one face; the two audio tracks are different lengths |
| `6263` | audio extremely low; camera moving a lot; one face partially covered; audio bleed between participants |

So these are **judgement calls that could be revisited**, not data loss. `4041` and
`6263` would likely still fail an OpenFace confidence floor, but `2425` is a
concatenation problem rather than a quality one and may be recoverable if the two
parts can be joined and re-synchronised.

Everything else is complete: **27 of 30 sessions have every stream, and the three
that do not have every *raw* stream.**

Note that `9899` appears in both `Study Sessions/` and `Pilot Sessions/`. The
version in `Study Sessions/` is the one that reaches the dataset.

### Study 3 — annotations stop dead at session 15

This is the single biggest gap in the project.

- **Behavioural data: 34 of 34 sessions complete.** Every session has audio, a
  Whisper transcript, and a transcript dataframe. 99 of 102 OpenFace streams were
  produced.
- **Annotation data: sessions 1–15 only.** Sessions 16–34 have no CORAE files on
  this drive at all — not a partial set, not a corrupted set, nothing. The cut is
  perfectly contiguous.

That pattern does not look like per-session failure. It looks like either
(a) annotation collection stopped after session 15 while recording continued, or
(b) the files for 16–34 exist on Cornell Box and were never copied down. **This is
worth resolving before anyone concludes the dataset is 15 sessions.** If it is (b),
the annotated set doubles to 34 sessions / 102 participants / 204 rating curves and
becomes a substantially more useful dataset than Study 2.

### Study 3 — three missing OpenFace streams

`3_C`, `17_A` and `21_A` have no OpenFace CSV. For `17_A` and `21_A` an
`_of_details.txt` exists without a CSV, meaning extraction was attempted and did not
complete. Session 3 is worst affected: `3_C` has no output and `3_A` tracks at 0.33
confidence, leaving only `3_B` usable — and session 3 **is** one of the annotated
ones, so it is a real loss rather than a cosmetic one.

Re-running `openface_run_all_vids.ps1` on those three clips is the obvious first fix.

The quality table also carries five rows that are not `<session>_<position>` —
`3_2`, `26`, `30`, `33`, `34` — which look like re-runs or whole-scene extractions.
They are counted in the 104 rows of
[`../results/study3/openface_confidence_per_participant.csv`](../results/study3/openface_confidence_per_participant.csv)
but are not part of the 102 participant streams.

## Open questions for the project team

1. **Do CORAE annotations exist for Study 3 sessions 16–34?** If so, where? This
   doubles the dataset.
2. **Were Study 3 mid-surveys and post-surveys collected?** The protocol specifies
   them, with laptops pre-loaded. No response file is on this drive, and there is no
   Study 3 equivalent of `ParticipantLog.xlsx`, so **no demographics exist for the
   102 Study 3 participants** on this drive. Study 2's personality and demographic
   findings cannot currently be replicated on Study 3.
3. **Is the Study 3 → seat-position mapping recorded anywhere?** Diarisation gives
   `SPEAKER_00/01/02`; cameras and CORAE give `A/B/C`. Without the mapping, audio
   features cannot be attributed to the right person.
4. **Should `2425` be recovered?** Its exclusion is a concatenation problem, not a
   quality one.
